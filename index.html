About Dr. Vujic

Dr. Ana Vujic is a cardiometabolic researcher at Placeholder University School of Medicine, where her lab studies why cardiovascular disease and Type 2 diabetes so often occur together — and what that overlap means for prevention and treatment.

Over the past fifteen years, her work has combined clinical cohort studies with laboratory research to better understand shared risk pathways, with the ultimate goal of developing screening tools and therapies that address both conditions at once.

  • Ph.D., Molecular Physiology — Placeholder University (2011)
  • M.D. — Placeholder School of Medicine (2007)
  • Postdoctoral Fellowship, Cardiometabolic Research — Placeholder Institute (2014)
  • Director, Cardiometabolic Research Lab — Placeholder University (2016–Present)

Research Focus

Heart Disease Research

Our lab studies the early biological signals that precede clinical heart disease, with an emphasis on how vascular inflammation and metabolic dysfunction interact over time.

Diabetes Research

We investigate insulin resistance and glucose regulation pathways to identify early markers of Type 2 diabetes risk and new avenues for intervention.
