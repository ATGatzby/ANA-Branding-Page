Postdoctoral Researcher

Placeholder bio. Alex joined the lab as a postdoctoral researcher, focusing on the early inflammatory signals that precede clinical heart disease. Their work centers on identifying blood-based biomarkers that could one day support earlier cardiovascular risk screening.

Before joining the lab, Alex completed doctoral research on endothelial cell signaling and its role in chronic vascular inflammation.
